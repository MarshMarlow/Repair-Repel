# Repair & Repel
Defend your castle from the evil monsters by balancing tasks in VR! During the game, you will have to balance the following tasks:
- Shooting zombies with the crossbow
- Blocking tomato projectiles with the shield
    - Repairing the crossbow and the shield with the anvil
- Equipping the headlamp to light up the battlefield
    - Recharging the headlamp when it runs out of battery
- Exorcising ghosts with the crucifix

### Controls
- Left/Right Thumb Trigger: **Grab object** (crossbow, shield, hammer, crucifix)
    - Hit crossbow/shield with hammer while on anvil: **Repair**
- Left/Right Index Trigger (*while holding crossbow*): **Shoot**
- B/Y (*while aiming at helmet*): **Equip Helmet**
    - B/Y (*with helmet equipped, aiming at recharge station*): **Unequip Helmet**
- A/X (*with helmet equipped*): **Toggle Headlight**
